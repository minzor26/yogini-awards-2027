ABOUT THE AWARDS

Celebrating the Spirit of the Yogini

Honouring Wisdom. Empowering Women. Inspiring Generations.

The International Yogini Awards celebrate women who dedicate their lives to yoga, spiritual practice, wellness, research, education, institutional leadership, and the service of society.

The awards provide a platform to recognize extraordinary contributions, connect inspiring women from across India and beyond, and encourage the next generation of Yoginis to lead with wisdom, compassion, and purpose.

OFFICIAL AWARDS

The Five Yogini Award Categories

Based on verified eligibility criteria from Bharatiya Yogini Sangh.

National & International

Shivani Award

Lifetime Achievement

Conferred upon venerable women aged 60 and above who have dedicated their entire lives to yoga and societal wellbeing.

Official Criteria: Women aged 60+ with lifelong dedication to yoga and spiritual service.
State & National Level

Gargi Award

Special Research & Scholarship

Dedicated to ancient philosopher Gargi Vachaknavi, honouring Yoginis conducting research and publishing papers in yoga and wellness.

Official Criteria: State Level: 5+ published papers. National Level: 10+ publications.
State & National Level

Yogini Award

Long-Time Practitioner

Recognizing dedicated Yoginis consistently practicing yoga for many years and uplifting society through their discipline.

Official Criteria: State Level: 10+ years practice. National Level: 15+ years practice.
State & National Level

Gonika Award

Institutional Yogini

Named after Gonika Maharshi (mother of Patanjali), presented to women who manage their own yoga centres, studios, or institutes.

Official Criteria: State Level: 10+ years active operation. National Level: 15+ years.
Public Recognition

Maitrayi Award

Yoga Well-Wishers & Supporters

Honouring women who actively champion, sponsor, and raise public awareness for yoga, preventive wellness, and personal health.

Official Criteria: Active support, patronage, volunteerism, or social advocacy for yoga.

CRITICAL ADVISORY • TRANSIT SCHEDULE

IMPORTANT TRAVEL INFORMATION FOR DELEGATES

Please review the mandatory transit schedule, venue transfer timing, and return ticket guidelines before planning your journey.

Phase 01
🛬

29 January – Arrival at Siliguri

All delegates are requested to reach Siliguri by the afternoon of 29 January.

🚗 Journey to Venue

After reaching Siliguri, delegates will proceed to the event venue. Please note that the journey from Siliguri to the venue takes approximately 4 hours.

Phase 02
🏔️

31 January – Gangtok Sightseeing

All delegates will stay at the venue on 31 January. Gangtok sightseeing will also be arranged on this day.

🛕 Venue Stay & Excursion

Delegates remain accommodated at the venue on 31 January, alongside the specially organized Gangtok sightseeing tour.

Phase 03
🛫

1 February – Departure

All delegates will leave the venue in the morning on 1 February and travel back to Siliguri.

⏱️ Morning Venue Departure

All delegates depart the venue in the morning for Siliguri (approx. 4 hours mountain transit).
